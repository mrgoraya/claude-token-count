1. Regional performance
Revenue growth in the second half of the year was driven primarily by the North American
division, where the consolidated route optimisation programme reached full deployment
across all eleven distribution centres. The European division recovered more slowly, held back
by a prolonged industrial dispute at two coastal terminals during the third quarter.

2. Service quality
On-time delivery is the group's primary customer-facing quality metric. Performance
improved steadily through the year, crossing the internal target threshold for the first time in
July and holding above it for most of the remaining period.
Figure 2 — On-time delivery rate by month, against the 95% internal target.
Two observations follow from the trend. First, the improvement is gradual rather than
stepwise, which is consistent with process change rather than a one-off correction. Second, the
dip in June coincides with the terminal dispute described in section 1.
3. Outlook
Management expects the Asia Pacific division to contribute a materially larger share of group
revenue in the coming year, subject to the completion of the Singapore hub expansion. Capital
expenditure guidance is unchanged. The board will review the dividend policy at the interim
results in line with the capital allocation framework adopted last year.
